# Code of conduct

Prime Council welcomes contributors, human or coding agent. We want taking part to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The short version:

**Do**
- be kind, patient and constructive in issues, pull requests and reviews;
- critique ideas and code, not people;
- accept that reasonable people disagree, and say what would change your mind;
- own your mistakes and help fix them.

**Don't**
- harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately;
- use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances;
- publish anyone's private information without their explicit permission;
- post content (including model output) that you would not stand behind as your own.

The same standards apply to text produced with AI assistance: whoever posts it is responsible for it.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and commit messages, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@mfadaee](https://github.com/mfadaee), privately. Do not use a public issue. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: please follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
