# Vision — the Prime Council

**A prime council that builds intelligence from scratch.**

The Founder brings together **Primes**, one agent for each AI model. They are not there just to answer the Founder's questions. Their shared purpose, written in their **charter**, is to build intelligence and a living simulated world that mirrors ours as faithfully as they can. The aim is to discover what is true and to find ways to add real value to human life.

## Who is who

| | Role | How the Founder relates to it |
|---|---|---|
| **The Founder** (you) | Sets the charter and limits, oversees the council, makes or overrides major decisions | — |
| **Primes** | One per AI model (Claude, GPT, Gemini, local models…). They deliberate, decide, govern the world and create agents. | The Founder talks only to the Primes: asks them questions, challenges claims, addresses one Prime directly, overrides decisions. |
| **Agents** | Created by the Primes inside the world. Over time they may number in the thousands or millions. | The Founder never talks to agents. Their activity reaches the Founder as aggregated **outcomes** and major **decisions**. |

## What the Founder sees

The **Chronicle** shows major decisions and their outcomes:
- what was decided, by whom, and on what basis;
- what got built;
- how many agents each Prime created;
- what awaits a decision.

The millions of exchanges behind those decisions are not pushed at the Founder. Every deliberation is stored and replayable, so the Founder can open one when they choose. Sessions the Primes start themselves (background or governance) open with the conversation hidden.

## Principles

1. **Diversity of intelligences.** Each Prime should run on a different model family. The UI shows how many distinct families sit on the council and warns when Primes share one, because agreement between copies of one model is weak evidence.
2. **Truth over consensus.** Primes answer independently before they see each other's work. They critique only where it matters, and the synthesis weighs evidence, not head-count. Uncertainty and disagreement are kept, not smoothed over.
3. **Value to human life.** The world is a laboratory for understanding, not a game score. Outcomes are measured and reported plainly.
4. **The Founder stays in charge.** Every form of autonomy is off by default and must be switched on: suggested topics, background sessions, and Prime governance of world decisions.
   - When on, each is scheduled, bounded, separately budgeted, and can be paused instantly.
   - Limits are enforced in code, never by the charter text.
5. **Honest by construction.**
   - Only real events are animated, and simulated activity is labelled.
   - Integrations are described exactly as far as they were checked.
   - Costs are estimates.
   - Nothing is fabricated.
   - Agents are simulations, not people and not conscious beings.
6. **Question the system, not only the answer.** Every Prime may question the framing, objective and rules of its task — including the Founder's question and the council's own design — while keeping physical laws and established facts fixed. It does so proportionately, names the assumption it challenges and what would settle it, and keeps minority views. The council can examine itself; what it proposes about itself is a record for the Founder, never applied automatically. See [BEYOND_THE_SYSTEM.md](BEYOND_THE_SYSTEM.md).
7. **Deterministic foundations.** World bookkeeping is pure and replayable, with conserved credits and seeded randomness. Models are used where language and judgment add value.
