# Product vision

**Simulated Life** is a place where you, the Prime, convene a council of distinct AI intelligences. The council slowly grows into a small, persistent simulated society that you can watch, question and steer.

## Principles

1. **Quality over consensus.** The council exists to produce the best supported answer to what you actually asked:
   - members answer independently before they see each other's work;
   - they critique only where it matters;
   - the synthesis weighs evidence over head-count.

   Agreement between models that share training data or sources is not proof.
2. **Honest by construction.**
   - Only real backend events are animated.
   - Simulated activity is always labelled.
   - Integrations are described exactly as far as they were checked.
   - Costs are estimates.
   - No fabricated data, sources, customers or history.
3. **The Prime decides.** Members propose; you approve. Autonomy is opt-in, scheduled, bounded, separately budgeted, and can be paused instantly.
4. **Safe defaults.**
   - The app runs locally; remote access requires a token.
   - Keys never leave the server.
   - Imported or retrieved content is untrusted data.
   - Limits are enforced in code, never by prompt.
5. **Deterministic where it can be.** World bookkeeping is pure and replayable. Models are called only where language or judgment adds value.
6. **Not people.** Members are AI models and inhabitants are simulations. The product never presents them as conscious beings or real people.

## The arc

| Stage | Experience |
|---|---|
| Council | Ask a question; watch distinct members deliberate in a living chamber; intervene; get a clear answer with evidence, uncertainty and how deliberation changed it. |
| Persistent intelligence | The council remembers what you approve, proposes discussions you'd value (with reasons), and can deliberate in the background within limits. |
| Living society | A small community with inhabitants, a fictional economy and projects. Big decisions go to the council, and its decisions change the world through explicit, replayable events. |
| Continuous improvement | The product improves itself one reviewed, tested change at a time. |

## Visual identity

- **The constellation:** distinct points of light joined into one ring around a central star, standing for separate intelligences forming a community.
- **Palette:** a deep night sky, with a luminous colour for each member and gold for the Prime.
- **State, not colour alone:** every state carries a text label and a glyph alongside its colour. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
